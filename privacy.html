Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  SKY GRIT

1. No Data Collection

FormPix does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or images. All image processing happens entirely inside your browser on your own device.

2. Your Images Stay With You

When you upload an image to FormPix, it is loaded directly into your browser's memory. It is never sent to any server. We have no technical ability to access, view, or store your images.

3. No Cookies or Tracking

FormPix does not use cookies, analytics trackers, or any third-party tracking scripts. We do not track your behaviour, device, or location.

4. No Account Required

FormPix requires no login, registration, or personal information of any kind to use.

5. Third-Party Services

FormPix is a standalone browser-based tool. It does not connect to any third-party APIs or services during image processing.

6. Children's Privacy

FormPix does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected at all, this service is safe for all ages.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
